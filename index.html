The Swabhiman Difference

Beyond housing —
a complete ecosystem

Seven pillars that set Nyati Swabhiman apart from every other developer in Pune.

Premium Homes

High-end 1, 2, 3 & 4 BHK residences designed for defence families — spacious, secure, and built for generations.

Strategic Location

Gated communities near military camps, command hospitals, CSD canteens and schools. 210-acre Nyati County township purpose-located.

Property Management

While you serve the nation, we safeguard your home. Professional management ensures maintenance and security in your absence.

24-Month Assured Rental

Our assured rental policy guarantees income when you're posted away. Financial security for your family, always.

Pick-up & Drop

Complimentary site visit transfers from Pune and Mumbai — explore your future home without any logistical hassle.

Preferential Pricing

No floor rise, no PLC. Exclusive pricing with special home finance schemes — fair value for those who serve.

Defence-Specific Services

From site-visit coordination to specialised home finance — every service tailored to defence personnel's unique needs.
